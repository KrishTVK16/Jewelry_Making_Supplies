Private Labeling

Detailed information about our premium jewelry services and how they benefit you.

Build Your Own Jewelry Brand

Our private labeling service is designed for designers and entrepreneurs who want to launch a jewelry line without the overhead of manufacturing. We handle everything from production to packaging, allowing you to focus on your brand identity.

You can choose from our extensive catalog of "white label" designs or work with us to modify existing pieces. We then package these items in your custom-branded boxes, pouches, or cards.

Key Benefits

  • Low Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)
  • Custom Packaging Sourcing
  • Logo Engraving Services
  • Fast Turnaround Times
  • Blind Shipping Options
  • Quality Assurance Guarantee
