Top Jewelry Trends for Spring 2026

Jan 24, 2026 Editorial Team Trends
Featured Image
Spring 2026 is seeing a resurgence of maximalism mixed with sustainable sourcing. Designers are looking for bold, chunky chains combined with delicate, traceable gemstones.

1. The Return of Yellow Gold

While white gold had its moment, 14k and 18k Yellow Gold are dominating the orders this season. It adds a warmth that complements the spring color palette perfectly. We're seeing a 40% increase in wholesale orders for yellow gold chains and findings.

2. Lab-Grown Dominance

Lab-grown diamonds and gemstones are no longer an alternative; they are the standard for the eco-conscious consumer. Our new catalog features an expanded range of lab-grown sapphires and emeralds that are chemically identical to mined stones but without the environmental impact.

"The modern jewelry buyer cares as much about the origin of the stone as they do about its clarity."

3. Chunky Chains & Layering

Layering isn't going anywhere. However, the texture is changing. Mixed link sizes and paperclip chains are being used to create statement pieces that can be worn alone or stacked. Wholesale buyers should stock up on varying gauges of chain.
