Our Legacy

Decades of Excellence in Jewelry Supply

Founded in 1998, Jewelora started as a small boutique supplier for local artisans. Today, we are a global pillar in the jewelry supply chain, connecting mines and manufacturers directly to designers.

We believe that great jewelry starts with great materials. That’s why we quality check every strand of pearls, every spool of wire, and every bag of clasps that leaves our warehouse.
